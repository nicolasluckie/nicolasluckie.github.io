Funeral Service Information System

A mobile-first, LAMP-stack CMS built for a funeral home, streamlining overnight dispatch and institutional access.

Overview

I designed and built this system while working as a funeral director's assistant, giving advisors, dispatchers, and partner institutions a single, mobile-friendly platform for managing daily operations.

It replaced paper-based dispatch logs and ad-hoc communication with a secure, location-aware tool purpose-built for overnight transfer and removal logistics.

Features

User Accounts & Management

Secure log-in and role-based account management for staff and partner institutions.

Institutional Profiles

Custom profiles for funeral homes, hospitals, nursing homes, and other institutions.

Cemetery Maps

Interactive maps for locating plots and navigating cemetery grounds.

On-Call Calculator

Automated scheduling tool for tracking and calculating on-call rotations.

Mobile Photo Upload

Field-ready photo capture and upload directly from mobile devices.

Gas Cards & Fleet Tracking

Tracking for fleet gas cards alongside vehicle usage records.

Toronto Police Divisional Map

Reference map of Toronto police divisions to support dispatch logistics.
